# Product photos

Put one photo per product here, named exactly as in the `image` column of the products CSV (for example `SR-001.jpg`).

- Free sources: Pexels or Unsplash (commercial use, no credit needed). Skip Unsplash+ images, photos with brand logos, and BlueChic's photos.
- Pick the photo first, then make the product's colours, fabric and description match it.
- About 800 px wide and under 200 KB each.

Until a photo exists, the shop shows fabric swatches in the product's colours.
